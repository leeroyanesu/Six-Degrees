# Audio Files

This directory contains placeholder references for audio files used in the scene intro.

## Required Audio Files:

1. **ambient-mystical.mp3**
   - Purpose: Background ambient music for mystical atmosphere
   - Suggested: Soft, ethereal tones, gentle synth pads, peaceful meditation music
   - Loop: Yes
   - Volume: Low to medium
   - Duration: 2-5 minutes recommended

2. **wind-ambiance.mp3**
   - Purpose: Wind and nature sounds for the floating island
   - Suggested: Gentle wind whooshing, occasional bird chirps, nature ambience
   - Loop: Yes
   - Volume: Low
   - Duration: 1-2 minutes recommended

3. **footsteps-grass.mp3**
   - Purpose: Character walking/footstep sounds
   - Suggested: Soft footsteps on grass or natural surface, not too heavy
   - Loop: Yes (will play continuously while character is moving)
   - Volume: Medium-low
   - Duration: 1-2 second loop recommended
   - Note: Should be seamless when looped

## Where to find royalty-free audio:

### General Sound Libraries:
- **Freesound.org** - Massive community library (requires attribution)
  - Search: "mystical ambient", "wind nature", "footsteps grass"
- **Pixabay** - No attribution required
  - Good for: ambient music, nature sounds
- **OpenGameArt.org** - Game-focused sounds
  - Excellent for: footsteps, ambient loops
- **YouTube Audio Library** - High quality, free
  - Good for: background music, ambient tracks
- **Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod)** - Royalty-free music
  - Great for: atmospheric background music

### Specific Recommendations:

**For Mystical Ambient Music:**
- Freesound: Search "meditation ambient" or "ethereal pad"
- Pixabay Music: Filter by "Ambient" and "Cinematic"
- YouTube Audio Library: "Ambient" category

**For Wind/Nature Ambiance:**
- Freesound: "wind ambience", "outdoor atmosphere", "birds chirping"
- Zapsplat.com: Nature sounds section (free with attribution)

**For Footsteps:**
- Freesound: "footsteps grass loop", "walking grass"
- OpenGameArt: Search "footstep" - many game-ready loops
- Zapsplat: Footsteps section with various surfaces
- Sonniss.com: Game Audio GDC bundles (free annually)

## Format:
- MP3 format preferred
- Sample rate: 44.1kHz or 48kHz
- Bitrate: 128-192 kbps sufficient for ambient sounds
- For footsteps: Ensure seamless loop (no clicks/pops at loop point)
